How to play charades

  1. Split into two teams. One person acts, everyone else guesses.
  2. No talking, no pointing at objects, no mouthing the word.
  3. Set a timer — 60 seconds is the classic. 30 makes it frantic.
  4. A correct guess scores a point. Pass if you are stuck.
  5. Most points when everyone has had a turn wins.
